Science first. Safety always.

This library is for general education. A chemical's risk depends on its concentration, amount, route and duration of exposure, and the way it is used. “Natural” does not automatically mean safe, and “synthetic” does not automatically mean harmful.

  • Check the product label and safety instructions.
  • Never mix household cleaning products.
  • Do not taste, smell closely, or experiment with unknown chemicals.
  • Use reliable references and qualified guidance for safety decisions.
